## Category 1: Number + Bold Claim

**Template:**
```
I [action] [specific number] [thing] in [timeframe].

[Here's how / You can have it for free / Here's what I learned]
```

**Fill-in examples:**
- I generated [X leads] in [30 days] using [one automation].
- I saved [12 hours/week] by replacing [manual task] with [tool].
- I grew from [X] to [Y followers] in [timeframe].

**Proven hooks to adapt:**
- "I blew up a LinkedIn account from 39 to 10,000 followers in just 17 days."
- "After 10,000+ hours of prompting, I built a single prompt library..."
- "8 tools I (religiously) use to do the work of 8 people"

## Category 2: Contrarian "Stop"

**Template:**
```
Stop [common behavior everyone does].

[Do this instead / Here's what actually works / Copy these X instead]
```

**Fill-in examples:**
- Stop manually [task]. Here's the automation that replaced it.
- Stop paying for [expensive thing]. This free alternative is better.
- Stop asking ChatGPT to "[vague request]." Use this prompt instead.

**Proven hooks to adapt:**
- "Stop embarrassing yourself with outdated tools."
- "Stop asking AI to summarise. Do this instead:"
- "Stop telling ChatGPT to 'write better.' Copy these 9 prompts instead."

**Proven hooks to adapt:**

## Category 4: Personal Transformation

## Quick Adaptation Framework

When adapting any hook for your voice:

1. **Replace vague → specific**
   - ❌ "saved a lot of time"
   - ✅ "saved 12 hours/week"

2. **Replace passive → active**
   - ❌ "Time was saved by using automation"
   - ✅ "I automated my entire client onboarding in 45 minutes"

3. **Add parenthetical personality**
   - ❌ "Tools I use daily"
   - ✅ "Tools I (actually) use daily"

4. **Make it about implementation**
   - ❌ "Why AI matters for business"
   - ✅ "The exact AI workflow that handles my invoicing"
