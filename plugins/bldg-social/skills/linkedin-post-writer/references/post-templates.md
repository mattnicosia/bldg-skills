## Formatting Reminders

Based on data from top performers:

- **Use arrows** (→ ↳ 👉) to guide the eye
- **Add parentheticals** for personality: "(actually)", "(religiously)", "(free)"
- **Include specific numbers** everywhere: 17 days, 10,000 followers, $16M
- **Target 1,400-1,800 characters** for optimal performance
- **Default to image posts** (70% of top 10% were images)
- **Always end with CTA stack** (85% of top performers did)

---

## Voice Adaptation Notes

When filling in these templates, apply your brand voice:

1. **Be specific** — "$47,329" not "good revenue"
2. **Be direct** — Skip "in today's world" and "many people don't realize"
3. **Be implementation-focused** — What did you DO, not what you think
4. **Be the smart friend** — Teaching, not lecturing
5. **Use your stories** — Brand Rocket, podcast guests, real client wins
