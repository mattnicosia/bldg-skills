## Key calibration decisions

1. **Fragility (2026-07-16).** "Serious" (0.75) only when NO mitigation designed.
   Designed but unproven mitigation = "minor" (0.9).

2. **Display multiplier (2026-07-16).** 1.12x maps internal scores to human-intuitive
   0-100 scale so the best ideas score 95-97, not 82-87. Purely cosmetic.

3. **Founder-Market Fit (2026-07-16).** Credit "incumbent constraint" — when incumbents
   can't make the same bet (architecture, cannibalization, inertia), the founders'
   willingness is itself an unfair advantage.

4. **Disruptor Lens (2026-07-16).** Pre-scoring classification (DISRUPTOR/REFORMER/HYBRID)
   changes how Propagation and Ignition are scored. No new math.

## Pattern: what 91+ ideas share

1. Product IS the proof. 10-second conversion. No explanation needed.
2. Incumbents structurally paralyzed. Can't respond without self-cannibalizing.
3. Founder IS the unfair advantage. The bet an incumbent couldn't make.

## Calibration method

To calibrate: pick a known successful company. Describe its founding concept as if
pitching it pre-launch. Score through KEYSTONE-7. If the score is far from reality
(e.g., Cursor at 65 old vs $100M actual), the instrument needs recalibration. Use
the most extreme gap as the calibration signal.
