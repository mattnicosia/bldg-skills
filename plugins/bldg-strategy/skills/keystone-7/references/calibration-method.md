# Calibrating KEYSTONE-7 Against Real Founding Concepts

The instrument's bands, fragility rules, and Founder-Market Fit guidance should be
re-tested periodically against real-world outcomes. Use this method.

## Method

1. **Pick 3-5 calibration concepts** spanning the range:
   - One obvious once-a-decade winner (Stripe, iPhone, ChatGPT)
   - One strong-but-not-obvious winner (Cursor, Lovable)
   - One surprising/counterintuitive winner (Polsia)
   - Your own concept(s) for comparison

2. **Score each at its founding moment** — not with the benefit of hindsight.
   What was known when the founders started building? What was uncertain?

3. **Compare scores to actual outcomes.** If a $500M ARR company scores below
   "build now," the bands are too strict. If a failed concept scores above "build
   now," the instrument is too forgiving.

4. **Adjust in this order:**
   - Fragility rules first (the Cursor test: if designed mitigations exist at
     founding, fragility should be minor (0.9), not serious (0.75))
   - Bands second (match natural clusters from real scores)
   - Display multiplier last (purely cosmetic; 1.12x maps 82-87 internal scores
     to 91-97 human-intuitive scores)
   - Individual dimension scoring guidance only if a clear pattern of mis-scoring
     emerges across multiple calibration concepts

5. **Never inflate scores to match outcomes.** The instrument measures structural
   soundness of the concept at founding. Execution can overcome a 69. Execution can
   squander a 95. The score is the concept, not the company.

## Key calibration decisions made

- **Fragility recalibration (2026-07-16):** "Serious" (0.75) reserved for weaknesses
  with NO designed mitigation. A designed, plausible mitigation — even unproven —
  qualifies as "Minor" (0.9). The Cursor test proved this: Copilot distribution
  risk had a designed mitigation (native editor > plugin), so 0.75 was wrong.
- **Bands recalibration (2026-07-16):** Shifted from 85/70/55 to 82/72/62 internal
  (then to 91/80/69 display after multiplier) to match natural clusters.
- **Founder-Market Fit guidance (2026-07-16):** Added "incumbent constraint" as a
  scoring signal — when incumbents cannot make the same bet because of architecture,
  cannibalization risk, or institutional inertia, the founders' willingness to make
  that bet is itself an unfair advantage.
- **Display multiplier (2026-07-16):** Added 1.12x multiplier so the best ideas in
  history land at 95-97, not 82-87. Purely cosmetic; does not affect math.
