# Montana Residential Estimate Conventions

Matt's standing instructions from E26049 (1004 Old Albany Post Rd, Garrison NY, Oct 2026). Apply to
Montana Contracting residential estimates in the Hudson Valley unless the drawings, specs or Matt say
otherwise on the job. Labor basis: open shop for the project's county. Rates are as of Oct 2026;
flag any that look stale on a later job instead of silently reusing them.

## Regrouping (one block per CSI subdivision)

- **01.00.00 General Requirements**: ONE block for all of Division 01 (supervision, PM, temp
  facilities, protection, blueprints, final cleaning). Keep **02.41.00 Waste Removal** as its own block.
- **06.11.00 Wood Framing** absorbs framing hardware (06.05), rough carpentry/blocking (06.10) and
  sheathing (06.16).
- **07.50.00 Roofing** absorbs flashing/sill pans (07.65), roof and wall penetrations (07.72),
  roofing insulation (rigid, tapered, protection board) and crane/hoisting.
- Own blocks: Foundation Waterproofing (07.11), Siding incl. soffit and rain screen (07.46),
  Roofing (07.50), Landscaping (32.90), Hardscaping (32.14), Drainage & Stormwater (33.40).
- **Below-grade insulation** (under slab, below-grade walls) goes in sitework (31.23.00).
- **Duct leakage and blower door tests** go in HVAC testing (23.05.93).
- **Drywall and Carpentry** is the block name for gypsum board work (09.21). Never "Gypsum Board Assemblies".
- **Architectural Metal and Glass** (08.40.00) is one block for aluminum and glass entry doors with their
  hardware, storefronts and aluminum windows (incl. aluminum sliding doors). Supply only.
- **Doors, Frames and Hardware** (08.11) holds only hollow metal or wood doors, frames and hardware, supply only.
- **Architectural Metal and Glass**: aluminum windows, sliders, storefront and aluminum doors are one furnished
  and installed line per item (supply and install combined).
- **No Finish Allowances block**: every line goes in its own trade category.
- **Excavation support** and adjacent building protection go in as an alternate unless definitely required. HM and wood doors, frames and hardware install in Finish
  Carpentry (06.20.00). Wood framers install residential windows only; that labor goes in Wood Framing.
- Residential windows stay in Windows (08.50), supply only.
- **No GC crane**: each trade carries its own hoisting (steel erector, framer, HVAC rigging, elevator, demolition).
- **Construction Waste Removal** (01.74.00) block sits above Demolition for construction containers; demolition
  containers stay in **Demolition** (02.41.00). 30 YD container $950/EA.
- Break structural steel into components (columns, girders/beams, braced frames, misc, base plates, connection
  design, erection crane) and balconies into framing, wall connection, deck, waterproofing, soffit, edge, drains.
- State R-value and thickness on every insulation line.
- Name floor finishes for what they are (e.g. "Polished Concrete", not "Polished Concrete or Epoxy").
  Polished concrete has no mil build; spec grind exposure, densifier and grit. Coatings are specified by mil thickness.
- Qualify flooring budgets: name the product (species, width, engineered or solid, prefinished or site
  finished; LVP wear layer), then split material allowance, labor and finishing lines.
- Exclude owner-supplied equipment (e.g. car lifts); carry power, anchor pads and coordination only.
- No Exterior Sealants & Firestopping line.
- No Material Markup line and no Insurance line. Markups: Design Contingency (TBD) at 0% above
  Overhead & Profit 15%.

## Standard exclusions

Temporary power, temporary heat, winter conditions (snow/ice, winter protection), rock hammering and
removal (offered as a day-rate alternate), removal/disposal of unsuitable soils, landscaping (unless
drawn), permits and fees, design/engineering, SWPPP preparation.

## Standard alternates (not in base)

Order them so the list opens with what the owner would want (a generator, upgrades) and puts what they
may be forced into (sprinkler, pump chamber, rock) after it. Matt: don't start the alternates with
things that would upset the owner.

Whole-house standby generator, carried complete (E26049, Oct 2026, $27,200 direct, $31,280 with O&P):
24 kW air-cooled propane unit with 200A service-rated ATS $8,500; electrical install with feeder,
controls, load management and startup $7,500; pad on stone $750; electrical trench ~40 LF at $20;
500 gal underground tank, owner-owned $3,000; excavate, bed, set and backfill the tank $2,250 (rock
excluded); gas trench ~50 LF at $20; gas piping, regulators and leak test $2,000; first fill allowance
$1,400. A leased tank from the propane supplier takes out the $3,000 but ties the owner to that supplier.

Building stakeout and foundation as-built; survey staking (house, well, septic); driveway grade
control layout; dewatering (if needed); rock removal at the sub's day rate ($3,000/DAY in Oct 2026) plus
O&P; fire sprinkler, septic pump chamber, whole-house standby generator when the site raises them (fire
access on a long steep drive, septic gravity in doubt, a remote all-electric house on a well). Say in the
alternate title what a generator covers (whole house or essential circuits).

## Driveways

- Do not assume asphalt. Carry asphalt only if a drawing calls for it; renders are not a call-out.
- Base = least-cost viable gravel drive: 10" RCA subbase (construction road) + 2" virgin Item 4
  wearing course at completion (~$60/CY delivered and spread) + regrade/crown/roll day + stone water
  bars on grades over ~10% (~every 50 LF, $450/EA allowance).
- Offer surface upgrades as net alternates: asphalt (full drive), tar and chip / double chip seal
  (~$3.50/SF), asphalt millings (~$1.10/SF over base; check Town/DEC near wells and parkland), stone-
  filled grid pavers on the steep run (~$8/SF), concrete on the steep run ($595/CY + $1.50/SF finish).
  Many upstate owners don't want asphalt; tar and chip is the usual upgrade.

## Envelope allowances held after research (Oct 2026)

- Extensive sedum tray green roof: $28/SF on ~1,200 SF (national $12-$20, small jobs ~$35).
- Wood siding: material allowance per architect ($14/SF + 10% waste on E26049), install on rain
  screen $10/SF, soffit install $12/SF, rain screen $1.50/SF. All-in runs high vs national.
- Imported European windows: $/SF from a comparable quote x FX x 1.25 dealer margin, plus 20%
  freight/duty/tariff and install separate.

## Radiant heat (offered as add alternates)

- Warmboard-S: panels F/O by HVAC (~$10/SF), tubing/manifolds by HVAC (~$4/SF), framer installs in
  lieu of subfloor (+$1.75/SF premium for layout and weight), deduct the subfloor material.
- Poured thin slab: gypsum thin slab over subfloor when the house is wood-framed over a crawl; add
  framing upsize and sleepers for nail-down wood floors.
- Both: combination boiler/water heater by HVAC replaces the HPWH, zone controls, wiring, a service
  upsize allowance for an electric boiler, and deduct any bath electric mats.

## Value engineering list Matt accepted for review on E26049

Domestic aluminum-clad windows in lieu of imported; EPDM only in lieu of green roof; LVL/PSL in lieu
of structural steel; schedule compression with windows, millwork, doors and the utility work order
finalized before breaking ground; lower siding material allowance; open-riser stair in lieu of a
custom floating stair; fixed screen panels in lieu of retractable; trimmed recessed in lieu of
tape-in. He rejected prefinished engineered flooring (keeps site-finished solid oak) and boulder
walls in lieu of concrete retaining walls.
