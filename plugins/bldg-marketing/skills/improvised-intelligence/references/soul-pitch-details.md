## 2. Loom Script Templates

### Structure (7 beats, ~85 seconds total)

1. Warm greeting (5 sec)
2. Curiosity gap / hook (10 sec)
3. Callback to conversation or context (15 sec)
4. The gift — what you built (20 sec)
5. Light insight — one specific finding (15 sec)
6. Value framing (10 sec)
7. Soft CTA (10 sec)

### Embedded Commands (weave naturally)

- "I want you to take a look at..."
- "When you see it laid out..."
- "When you're ready..."
- "If you want to really help me help you..."
- "You'll notice..."
- "You'll see..."

### Template A: Warm Lead (Met in Person)

```
Hey [Name]! Happy [relevant greeting]!

So... what if your [pain point] didn't have to be [negative state]? What if the
right [customers] were finding you and saying yes before you even got on the phone?

I kept thinking about what you said at [location]. About [pain point 1]. About
[pain point 2]. And I told you I'd put something together.

So I want you to take a look at what I built. I went through everything —
[platform 1], [platform 2], your website — and I mapped out how to close that gap.
When you see it laid out, it's going to click.

There's a 90-day content plan in there. It's yours — whether we ever work together
or not. [Seasonal] gift from me to you.

When you're ready, book a call. Or just text me. And if you want to really help
me help you, answer those few questions at the bottom.

Talk soon!
```

**Tone:** Like FaceTiming a new friend. Reference specific details. Casual, genuine, zero pressure.

### Template B: Warm Lead (Referral)

```
Hey [Name]! I'm Dennison — [Referrer] mentioned you might be open to a conversation.

So... what if [pain point] didn't have to be [negative state]?

I took a deep look at [Business Name] — your [platform], your reviews, your
online presence — and I put together some thoughts.

Here's what I found: You've built something real. [Specific stat]. But there's a
gap between the experience you deliver and how you're showing up online. That gap?
It's probably costing you [bookings/clients/revenue].

I mapped it all out — the opportunities, the search landscape, and a 90-day
content plan you can take whether we ever talk again or not.

Link's in [the email / the message]. Take a look. And if any of it resonates,
book a quick call.

Talk soon.
```

**Tone:** Friendly but slightly more professional. Establish credibility faster.

### Template C: Cold Outreach

```
Hey [Name] — I'm Dennison from Blues Prince Media. You don't know me yet, but
I spent some time going through [Business Name]'s online presence. And I have
some thoughts.

Here's what I noticed: You've got [positive observation]. But when [customers]
search for [service] in [location]... you're not always showing up.

That's a visibility problem. And visibility is solvable.

I put together a breakdown — the gaps, the opportunities, and a 90-day content
plan specific to your business. It's yours whether we ever talk or not.

Link's below. Take 15 minutes. If it resonates, there's a link to book a quick call.

Hope to hear from you.
```

**Tone:** Confident, not arrogant. Lead with value, not pitch. Pattern interrupt early.

### Recording Tips
- Look at camera, not screen
- Natural lighting (face a window)
- Slight smile at greeting
- Lean in when delivering key insight
- End with warmth, not sales pressure

### 90-Day Content Calendar Structure

- Specific post ideas with hooks
- Hashtag strategy

### Brand Positioning Worksheet Sections
1. Your Business (what, differentiator, transformation)
2. Your Ideal Customer (dream customer, search terms, objections)
3. Your Brand Voice (3 words, anti-words, formal vs. casual)
4. The Practical Stuff (slow season, services to promote, existing assets)
5. Anything Else (approach feedback, 90-day success definition)

### Market Snapshot Structure
- Header stats row: Rating, Review count, Years in business, Industry-specific metric
- Section 1: What You've Built (acknowledge accomplishments)
- Section 2: The Visibility Gap (experience vs. online presence)
- Section 3: What Customers Are Searching (terms + volumes)
- Section 4: Your Competitive Edge (competitors + unique lane)
- Section 5: Bottom Line (tie to pain point, offer solution)
