# CSI Master Format Subdivisions Reference

The SOW is organized by 6-digit CSI MasterFormat subdivisions, not top-level divisions. This is the level at which subs typically bid and at which scope is most clearly understood.

Use the format: `## ## ## — Subdivision Title` as the header for each subdivision section in the SOW.

## How to Choose the Right Subdivision

When extracting items from drawing.md files, map each item to the most specific subdivision available. If multiple subdivisions could apply, use the one that matches the trade most likely to bid that line item.

If a subdivision is genuinely needed but not in this list, use the closest standard MasterFormat code from the appropriate division.

## Cross-Trade Routing Rules

| Item Type | Furnish Subdivision | Install Subdivision |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Hollow Metal Doors/Frames | 08 11 13 (F/O) | 06 20 00 / 09 22 16 (I/O) |
| Wood Doors | 08 14 00 (F/O) | 06 20 00 (I/O) |
| Door Hardware | 08 71 00 (F/O) | 06 20 00 (I/O) |
| Toilet Accessories | 10 28 13 (F/O) | 06 20 00 / 09 29 00 (I/O) |
| Bathroom Accessories | 10 28 23 (F/O) | 06 20 00 / 09 29 00 (I/O) |
| Plumbing Fixtures (F/O) | 22 40 00 (F/O) | 22 11 00 / 22 13 00 (I/O Rough-In) |
| Light Fixtures (interior) | 26 51 13 (F/O) | 26 00 00 Electric (I/O) |
| Light Fixtures (exterior/landscape) | 26 56 00 (F/O) | 26 00 00 Electric (I/O) |
| Plumbing Fixtures (each fixture) | 22 40 00 (F/O) | 22 00 00 Plumbing (I/O + rough-in) |
| Millwork (interior doors, shutters, shelving, medicine cabinets, built-in seating, veneer panels) | 06 41 00 Millwork (F/O if GC-supplied; owner-furnished = no GC furnish line) | 06 41 00 Millwork (I/O) |
| Casework (kitchens, closets, wardrobes, wet bars) | 12 32 00 Casework | 12 32 00 Casework (owner furnish+install = Tenant; GC does MEP connections) |
| Owner-Supplied Equipment | (No furnish line) | Appropriate trade subdivision (I/O Owner-Supplied) |

Notes:
- Millwork is NOT carpentry: keep all millwork in 06 41 00, never 09 22 16. Millwork doors go in Millwork (override the door rule above).
- Casework (12 32 00) is distinct from millwork and from appliances; list each casework type as its own line.
- Slab toppings route by material: Gyp-Crete -> 03 54 00 Cast Underlayment; concrete -> 03 30 00; finish floor/decking -> 09 6x. Plywood subfloor stays in 09 22 16.
- Existing-condition verbs (Reuse/Restore/Repair/Protect/Salvage/Rebuild/Relocate) use `Provide` or `Remove`, never F/O or I/O.
| Blocking | 06 10 00 (Rough Carpentry) only — never to MEP trades |
